# Universal Orlando: products, price drivers and what to verify

This is background for FionaAI. **Rules and prices change often.** Treat everything here as a checklist of what to look up, not as facts to quote. Confirm each item on the official site for the client's dates.

## Parks and calendar
- **Universal Studios Florida** (`usf`), **Islands of Adventure** (`ioa`) and **Epic Universe** (`epic`, opened in 2025, with its own entrance away from the other two) are the theme parks. **Volcano Bay** is the water park, where rides use a virtual line (TapuTapu). **CityWalk** is a free dining and entertainment area.
- The Wizarding World of Harry Potter is split between Diagon Alley (USF) and Hogsmeade (IOA), linked by the **Hogwarts Express**, which needs a Park-to-Park ticket. Epic Universe has its own Ministry of Magic area.
- Hours change by date. Check each park day, and check refurbishments of must-dos.
- **Events**: Halloween Horror Nights (selected nights in Sept–Oct, separate ticket, very scary, not for kids), Mardi Gras (around Feb–Apr), and holiday events (Nov–Dec). Busy event nights can change park hours and crowds.

## Tickets
- **1-park per day** or **Park-to-Park** (both USF and IOA the same day). Multi-day tickets get cheaper per day. Check how many consecutive days they cover and how Epic Universe is included: a separate dated Epic Universe ticket, or a multi-day ticket that includes one Epic day (verify for the dates).
- Prices are dynamic and date-based. Children's ticket ages differ from Disney's (verify). Children under 3 enter free.
- Volcano Bay: separate day ticket, or included in some multi-day tickets.

## Hotels
- **Premier**: Loews Portofino Bay, Hard Rock Hotel, Loews Royal Pacific. Each guest gets **Universal Express Unlimited at USF and IOA**, from check-in day through check-out day (verify; not valid at Epic Universe or Volcano Bay). Boats and walking paths to the parks.
- **Preferred**: Loews Sapphire Falls, Universal's Aventura.
- **Prime Value**: Cabana Bay Beach Resort (family suites), Endless Summer Resort (Surfside and Dockside), and Stella Nova / Terra Luna near Epic Universe (verify tier and perks).
- **Helios Grand** (Epic Universe): a hotel with direct entry into Epic Universe (verify its perks).
- Most Universal hotels include **Early Park Admission** (usually one hour before opening at a selected park; verify which park each day) and free shuttles or boats.
- Price with taxes for the exact nights and a room type that fits the party (Cabana Bay family suites sleep 5–6).

## Universal Express
- **Express Unlimited**: use the Express line on each eligible ride as often as you like, that day. **Express (once)**: one Express ride per eligible ride. It's sold per park or park-to-park per day, with dynamic prices, and it can sell out.
- **Not included**: Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure, some new rides, and Epic Universe (it has its own Express product with limited availability; verify).
- Compare: Express for every person for every day versus upgrading to a Premier hotel. For 2+ nights and 3+ people, the hotel often wins.

## Accessibility
- **Attraction Assistance Pass (AAP)**: for guests who can't wait in a standard line. It needs the **IBCCES Individual Accessibility Card (IAC)**, requested online before the trip. Explain the process; never judge eligibility.
- Many rides are intense simulators or coasters with tight restraints, so check each traveler's flags with `ta park-info`. **Child Swap** is available on rides with a height limit.

## Dining and extras
- Character dining, Harry Potter dining (Three Broomsticks, Leaky Cauldron), CityWalk restaurants, Butterbeer.
- Optional upsells: interactive wands, the photo package, VIP Experience tours, Volcano Bay cabanas, Halloween Horror Nights, and the Universal dining plan if offered.

## Transfers
- From MCO: private transfer, a shuttle, rideshare or a rental car. Universal hotels charge for parking. Between Universal and Disney there's no free shuttle, so price a transfer for a combined trip.

## Verify every time
- [ ] Park hours and Early Park Admission for each date
- [ ] Ticket price for the exact dates and days, with or without Park-to-Park, and how Epic Universe is included
- [ ] Hotel price with taxes for the exact nights and room, and whether its Express perk applies to these dates
- [ ] Express Unlimited / once price per park day, and Epic Universe Express availability
- [ ] Refurbishments of must-dos; HHN or event nights on the dates
- [ ] Current children's ticket age range
- [ ] Any promotion (ticket deals like "buy 2 days get more free", hotel offers) and its conditions
