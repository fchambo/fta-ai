# Question bank

Pick the blocks that apply, then trim. ★ = usually needed to quote.

## Core (always)
- ★ Who is traveling? Names as they appear on the passport, and ages of anyone under 18
- ★ Exact or flexible dates? How many nights? How flexible (± days)?
- ★ Departure city or airport
- ★ Budget: total or per person? Does it include flights?
- What's the occasion or main goal of the trip?
- Travel style: comfort, value or luxury? Packaged or independent?
- Accommodation preferences: hotel category, apartment, all-inclusive, location vs. price
- Flights: preferred airline or alliance, loyalty numbers, cabin class, max layovers
- Nationality and passport expiry date for each traveler
- Health, mobility or dietary needs we should plan around
- Travel insurance: do you already have it (credit card, annual policy)?
- Have you been there before? What did you love or dislike?
- How do you prefer to receive the proposal, and by when?

## Beach / resort
- All-inclusive or room only/breakfast?
- Quiet adult-friendly or lively with activities?
- Beachfront essential? Sargassum or rainy-season sensitivity?
- Interest in diving, snorkeling or excursions?

## City / cultural
- Pace: packed sightseeing or slow travel?
- Guided tours or self-guided? Private or shared?
- Must-see places, museums, shows or restaurants to book ahead?
- Walking tolerance per day?

## Adventure / nature
- Fitness level and experience (trekking, altitude, diving certification)
- Gear they own vs. need to rent
- Comfort with basic lodging, camping or long transfers?

## Cruise
- Interior, ocean view, balcony or suite?
- Past cruise lines and loyalty status
- Pre/post-cruise nights at the port city?
- Shore excursions through the cruise line or independent?

## Ski / snow
- Skill level for each traveler; lessons needed?
- Ski-in/ski-out important? Equipment rental and passes included?

## Safari / wildlife
- Must-see animals or migration timing?
- Lodge style: tented camp or luxury lodge? Shared or private vehicle?
- Fly-in vs. drive between parks? Children's age limits matter here

## Honeymoon / romance
- Wedding date (some resorts require proof for perks)
- Relaxation vs. adventure split
- Special touches (private dinner, spa, upgrades)

## Family with kids
- Ages, cribs, connecting rooms, kids' club
- Stroller or car seats? Nap/schedule constraints?
- For minors traveling without both parents: permission letter/notarized consent

## Seniors / accessibility
- Wheelchair or assistance at airports
- Accessible rooms, stairs, walking limits
- Medication storage, nearby medical facilities

## Business / bleisure
- Who pays which part (company vs. personal)? Invoice details
- Meeting locations, lounge access, flexible fares

## Group
- Group size, decision maker and payer(s)
- Single vs. shared rooms
- Deposit and name-change deadlines everyone must meet
