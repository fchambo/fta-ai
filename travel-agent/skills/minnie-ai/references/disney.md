# Walt Disney World: products, price drivers and what to verify

This is background for MinnieAI. **Rules and prices change often.** Treat everything here as a checklist of what to look up, not as facts to quote. Confirm each item on the official site for the client's dates.

## Parks and calendar
- Four theme parks: Magic Kingdom (`mk`), EPCOT (`epcot`), Hollywood Studios (`hs`), Animal Kingdom (`ak`). Two water parks: Typhoon Lagoon and Blizzard Beach, usually one open at a time, with seasonal closures. Disney Springs (free shopping and dining area).
- **Hours change by date.** Animal Kingdom usually closes earliest. Check each park day.
- **Magic Kingdom party nights** (Halloween party, roughly Aug–Oct; Christmas party, roughly Nov–Dec): MK closes early for day guests, and the party is a separate ticket. Use it as an optional upsell, and keep MK headliner days off party nights.
- EPCOT festivals (International Festival of the Arts, Flower & Garden, Food & Wine, Festival of the Holidays) bring extra crowds on weekends but are a selling point.
- Refurbishments and closures: check for each must-do.

## Tickets
- **Date-based**: the price depends on the start date and the number of days. Multi-day tickets have to be used within a window (verify how long). The price per day drops as days are added.
- **Park Hopper** (and similar add-ons): lets the group visit more than one park a day. It's worth it for longer stays, adult groups, or EPCOT evenings. It's often not needed for families with small kids.
- Water Park & Sports option, or separate water park tickets.
- Ages: Disney's child ticket age range differs from Universal's (verify the current range). Children under 3 enter free.
- Look up the price for the **exact dates and number of days** on the official ticket calendar.

## Hotels (Disney resorts)
- **Value**: All-Star resorts, Pop Century, Art of Animation (family suites). **Moderate**: Caribbean Beach, Coronado Springs, Port Orleans French Quarter / Riverside, and cabins at Fort Wilderness. **Deluxe**: Contemporary, Polynesian, Grand Floridian (monorail); Wilderness Lodge, Animal Kingdom Lodge, Beach Club / Yacht Club, BoardWalk; plus Disney Vacation Club villas such as the Riviera.
- **Perks** to check for the dates: **Early Entry** (30 min before opening at every park, for Disney resort guests and some partner hotels), **Extended Evening Hours** (Deluxe and DVC, select nights), **booking Lightning Lane 7 days before check-in** for the whole stay (off-site guests book 3 days before each day), dining reservations for the whole stay, transportation (buses, Skyliner, monorail, boats), and parking fees.
- Room capacity matters for families of 5+: look for family suites, cabins or two rooms.
- Prices: quote with taxes for the exact nights. Note any room-only or package offer and its conditions (deposit, cancellation).

## Lightning Lane
- **Multi Pass**: per park and per day. Pre-book return times for a set of attractions, in tiers at some parks (verify the tier rules per park). The price changes by date and park.
- **Single Pass**: one ride on one headliner that isn't in Multi Pass (often the newest coasters or most popular rides). The price changes by date.
- **Premier Pass**: one entry on each eligible attraction, for a premium price, with limited availability (verify parks and dates).
- Patty suggests Lightning Lane return times. Booking windows: 7 days before check-in for Disney resort guests, 3 days before the park day for everyone else, both at 7:00 a.m. Orlando time (verify).

## Accessibility
- **DAS (Disability Access Service)** is mainly for guests who can't wait in a standard line because of a developmental disability such as autism. Registration is by video chat before the trip (within the current window) or at the park. Explain the process; never judge eligibility.
- Wheelchairs and ECVs can be rented in the park, or delivered by off-site companies (often cheaper for multi-day). Rider Switch is available on most rides with a height limit.

## Dining and extras
- Dining reservations open 60 days ahead, and Disney resort guests can book their whole stay at once (verify). Character meals sell out at popular times.
- **Disney Dining Plan**: check whether it's offered for the dates and whether it saves money for this family. It usually doesn't for small eaters.
- Optional upsells: parties, Disney After Hours, Memory Maker (photos), dessert parties and fireworks viewing, tours, Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique, and a celebration button or room setup.

## Transfers
- Disney's Magical Express no longer runs. From MCO: private transfer, a shuttle service, rideshare or a rental car. Price the option that fits the group (car seats for small kids).

## Verify every time
- [ ] Park hours and Early Entry / Extended Evening for each date
- [ ] Ticket price for the exact start date and number of days, with or without Park Hopper
- [ ] Hotel price with taxes for the exact nights and room type
- [ ] Lightning Lane Multi Pass, Single Pass and Premier Pass prices for each park day
- [ ] Refurbishments of must-dos, and party nights at Magic Kingdom
- [ ] Current child ticket age range
- [ ] Any promotion (room discounts, ticket deals) and its conditions
